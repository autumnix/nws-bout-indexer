Bout Post-Processing Specs
==========================

1. CHAPTER TIMESTAMP PLACEMENT
   - Jam 1 of each period: chapter starts at the earliest frame where the jam timer
     shows 2:00 under J1.
   - Jam N (N>1): chapter starts at the first frame where the new jam number J<N>
     and the LINEUP banner appear together.
   - Jams following a timeout: chapter starts when the POST TIMEOUT banner first
     appears. The jam number won't be shown during POST TIMEOUT but will appear
     once the jam begins.

2. TIMEOUT CHAPTERS
   - Official Timeout, Team Timeout, Timeout, and any other text that appears in
     the red/colored banner are each separate chapter entries.
   - Chapter timestamp is when the timeout banner first appears on the scoreboard.

3. SCOREBOARD LAYOUT
   - Right side: Period (P<N>), Jam (J<N>), period clock, jam clock, and state
     banners (LINEUP, COMING UP, TEAM TIMEOUT, OFFICIAL TIMEOUT, POST TIMEOUT,
     HIGHLIGHT, etc.).
   - Left side: Team names, scores, and player names. The first (left-most) column
     after the score shows the Jammer (marked with a star). Subsequent columns show
     blockers/pivot.
   - Period clock is directly under P<N> (and any banners). It is to the LEFT of the
     jam clock.

4. HIGHLIGHT FRAMES
   - During a jam, a HIGHLIGHT banner can overlay the scoreboard. These frames must
     be skipped when reading names or scoreboard state. Seek past them to find the
     actual data.

5. PLAYER NAME READING
   - The Jammer is always the first (left-most) name and will have a star at the
     start of a jam. Mid-jam the star might move to a blocker if the jammer passes
     to the pivot.
   - Names may load late. Seek forward through frames during the jam to find when
     names appear. Use the earliest chapter timestamp but the later frame for names.
   - The number of visible player columns varies by bout/software configuration.
     Common configurations:
       Jammer* | Pivot
       Jammer* | Pivot | Blocker1 | Blocker2 | Blocker3
   - If a jammer name stays empty but a pivot is shown, render as:
       <Team>: * | <Pivot Name>
   - If a name column stays empty for the entire jam, omit it (no placeholder).

6. CHAPTER FORMAT
   Period <N>:
   HH:MM:SS P<N>J<NN> [<Period Clock as NNmNNs>]
   <Team 1>: <Jammer>* | <Blocker 1>
   <Team 2>: <Jammer>* | <Blocker 1>

   HH:MM:SS <Timeout Type> [<Period Clock as NNmNNs>]

   HH:MM:SS P<N>J<NN> (Post Timeout) [<Period Clock as NNmNNs>]
   <Team 1>: <Jammer>* | <Blocker 1>
   <Team 2>: <Jammer>* | <Blocker 1>

   - Jam numbers are zero-padded (01, 02, etc.) for column alignment.
   - Jams following a timeout are annotated with (Post Timeout).
   - Period clock value is read from the scoreboard at the chapter timestamp.

7. SAMPLING
   - Extract frames every 3 seconds; sufficient to catch all transitions within
     +/-3s accuracy.

8. OCR DATA STORAGE
   - Store intermediate OCR data in a named folder (e.g., "Game 1 OCR/") for
     easier reprocessing.
   - Suggested subfolders:
       right/   - right scoreboard crops (P/J, state, period clock)
       left/    - left scoreboard crops (player names)
